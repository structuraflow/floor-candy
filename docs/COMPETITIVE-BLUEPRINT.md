# Competitive Blueprint — NSW Coastal Epoxy Flooring

Reverse-engineered homepage structure for this niche, derived from live scrapes of the sites that actually rank for Newcastle, the Central Coast, the Mid North Coast and Sydney.

**Researched 21 September 2026.** 8 competitors scraped in full, 3 underperformers scraped for contrast, 13 homepage positions counted, plus a direct HTTP performance probe on every homepage in the set.

Visual deliverable: `~/Desktop/site-blueprint-nsw-coastal-epoxy-flooring.html`

**Nothing in this document changes a claim on the site.** It is research. Where it identifies a gap that could only be closed by asserting something Floor Candy cannot yet evidence, that gap is marked **blocked** and cross-referenced to `QUESTIONS-FOR-JARROD.md`.

## How the contender list was built

Four commercial-intent searches — "best epoxy flooring Newcastle", "epoxy flooring Central Coast reviews", "garage floor epoxy Port Macquarie / Taree / Forster", "best epoxy flooring companies Australia" — then filtered.

Rejected: directories (hipages, Localsearch, Yelp, BBB) as contenders rather than sources; resin and equipment suppliers (Epoxy Warehouse, Sydney Industrial Coatings, XPS); social profiles; and DIY content.

Ranked by frequency across searches, commercial-intent position, and review volume.

## The blueprint — 13 positions, counted

Frequencies are across the eight primary contenders only.

| # | Position | Freq | Band |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hero — geo-qualified H1 + phone + dual CTA | **8/8** | Universal |
| 02 | Credential strip immediately under hero | 6/8 | Majority |
| 03 | Inline quote form, high on the page | 6/8 | Majority |
| 04 | Proof-number bar (floors · years · rating · radius) | 5/8 | Majority |
| 05 | Services grid, split residential / commercial | **8/8** | Universal |
| 06 | Preparation as the differentiator | 6/8 | Majority — but **4/4 of the best sites** |
| 07 | Numbered process, five steps | 4/8 | Split |
| 08 | **Paired before / after with job specs** | **2/8** | **Open goal** |
| 09 | Reviews — named, dated, ideally live | **8/8** | Universal |
| 10 | Service-area suburb list | **7/8** | Universal |
| 11 | FAQ — price, slip, duration, lifespan | 4/8 | Split |
| 12 | Final CTA with phone repeated | **7/8** | Universal |
| 13 | Footer credentials — licence · ABN · insurance | 4/8 | Split |

**Only five positions clear 7/8.** This niche has a smaller universal core than most local-service categories — the separation happens in the majority band, where the best sites do things the rest skip entirely.

### Position 01 — the hero

Not one winner runs a generic hero. Service and geography both sit in the H1 or the eyebrow directly above it, the mobile number is a tappable `tel:` link in both header and hero, and there are exactly two CTAs — a form and a call. Never three.

Verbatim:

- Ironclad — *"Epoxy Flooring in Sydney, Built on Preparation First"*
- Dynamic — *"Epoxy Flooring Central Coast — Commercial & Residential Specialists"*
- Floortex — *"Newcastle's Epoxy Flooring Specialists: Residential, Garage & Commercial"*

Against Terry's Epoxy, which opens with "Get a free quote", and Nu Look, which opens with "Premium Epoxy Flooring Specialist" — no town, no claim, nothing to disagree with.

### Position 06 — preparation is the whole argument

The beat that sorts the field. Every site that ranks *and* converts has a dedicated section arguing the coating is the easy part. The two sites without it are the two weakest in the set.

- Ironclad — *"The Job Is Won or Lost in the Preparation"*, and names the spec: diamond grinding to **CSP 2–3**, moisture testing, structural crack repair, penetrating primer
- Dynamic — runs it as **problem agitation before any service is mentioned**: *"Most epoxy jobs fail within 3 years. Here's why."* Three named failure modes — cheap coatings, subcontractors who cut corners, one-size-fits-all systems
- Floortex — *"This is the step most people don't see, but it's the most important"*

All of them frame prep as the corner cheap operators cut, which pre-loads the buyer to distrust the next, cheaper quote they receive.

Against ECE Epoxy Crew, which spends ~1,400 words on "the Mid North Coast's spectacular coastal lifestyle" and never once describes preparing a slab.

### Position 08 — paired before/after: the open goal

Six of eight have a "gallery" — finished floors only, no before, no context, frequently no location. A finished epoxy floor is indistinguishable from any other finished epoxy floor. **The transformation is the product, and they photograph everything except it.**

Only Ironclad and Dynamic show the same floor twice. Only Ironclad captions the pair:

- A **drag slider** per job, proving the two frames are the same camera position
- Caption carrying **system · square metres · suburb** — "Flake epoxy · 64m² · Baulkham Hills"
- One line on what was actually wrong — *"Old stained garage slab ground back, oil contamination removed"*
- A testimonial repeating the same spec — "Baulkham Hills · 64m² flake epoxy double garage" — so photo and quote corroborate each other

Against Nu Look's four un-captioned Facebook exports, and Top Epoxy's portfolio, which carries nine tiles reading **"26 Oct 2025 Category / Project Name"** — placeholder text live on the page, under 188 five-star reviews.

### Position 10 — the suburb list

Least designed block on every site, most directly responsible for local ranking. Plain text, twenty to thirty named towns — not a map, not an accordion, not an image. Split by crew or region where there's more than one base, which doubles as a capability claim. Closed with a permission line: *"Not sure if we cover your area? Give us a call."*

The strongest sites back it with individual location pages — Ironclad one per Sydney suburb, Dynamic seven, Floortex eleven.

### Position 11 — the price question

Four sites run an FAQ; all four answer the same four questions (lifespan, slip, duration, cost). **Two answer the cost one with a real number, and both rank top-three in their own geography:**

- Floortex — *"residential epoxy flooring in Newcastle typically ranges from $50 to $120 per square metre for a full system including preparation and top coat"*
- Dynamic — *"Residential garages typically start from $90–$100 per m². The price we quote is the price you pay, no variations on the day."*

Both pair the range with a free on-site, fixed-written-price promise, so the range never hardens into a quote.

Dynamic is also the only site in the entire set that **publishes work it refuses to do**: *"We do not apply epoxy over tiles, timber, or vinyl. Those surfaces flex, expand, and contract in ways that cause epoxy to delaminate and fail."* Sydney Epoxy Floors sells epoxy-over-tiles as a headline service. One of those reads as expertise.

## Measured performance

Single cold fetch per origin, 21 Sep 2026, no browser rendering — delivery figures, not Core Web Vitals.

| Site | Stack | TTLB | HTML | Scripts | Stylesheets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor Candy** | Static HTML | 0.03s (local) | **80 KB** | **1** | **1** |
| Dynamic Flooring | Next.js | 0.22s | 78 KB | 9 | 1 |
| Sydney Epoxy Floors | Havealook CMS | 0.31s | 53 KB | 12 | 2 |
| Ironclad Floors | Static / SSG | 0.31s | 186 KB | 10 | 1 |
| Floortex Industries | WP + Elementor | 0.32s | 250 KB | 36 | 39 |
| Complete Coatings | WP + Elementor | 0.71s | 142 KB | 28 | 33 |
| Nu Look Floors | WP + Elementor | 0.83s | **696 KB** | 33 | **134** |
| ECE Epoxy Crew | WP + WP Rocket | **16.92s** | 340 KB | 34 | 33 |

Top Epoxy returned 403 to the probe (bot protection) and is excluded.

**Three conclusions:**

1. **Nu Look ranks #1 organically for "epoxy flooring Newcastle" on a 696 KB page with 134 stylesheets.** Ranking in this niche is currently won on links and age, not speed — which means speed is available as an advantage nobody is defending.
2. **ECE Epoxy Crew took 16.9 seconds.** A Mid North Coast competitor effectively unusable on a phone on regional mobile data — the exact context most of these searches happen in.
3. Only Dynamic and Ironclad are on a modern stack, and they are also the two best-structured sites in the set. The teams who rebuilt properly also rewrote properly.

> **Note on measuring our own live domain:** `curl` to `www.floorcandyepoxy.com` fails from this machine with `SSL certificate problem: unable to get local issuer certificate`, and plain HTTP returns a 403 from an intercepting proxy. **This is local TLS interception, not an outage.** The live site was verified healthy via Firecrawl the same day. Use Firecrawl, not curl, to check the live domain from here.

## The direct threat: Floortex Industries

The single most important competitive fact in this research.

Floortex is **not** a Newcastle company that occasionally travels north. It runs **two crews, one based in Taree**, and its service-area block names our towns individually:

> Taree · Forster · Tuncurry · Harrington · Wingham · Old Bar · Manning Valley · Gloucester · Bulahdelah · Tea Gardens · Hawks Nest · Port Macquarie

It also carries: 10+ years, a published $50–120/m² range, a six-question FAQ, a five-step process with timing chips, eleven location pages, a Google review embed, and an explicit "no subcontractors, two in-house crews" claim.

**Where it is beatable:** proof quality and speed. Its gallery is uncaptioned and unpaired, its three visible Google testimonials are 3–5 years old, and it ships 250 KB with 36 scripts and 39 stylesheets.

**Where it is not:** tenure, crew size, review volume, location-page coverage. Do not try to compete on those — and note the standing constraint that Floor Candy may use subcontractors, so the "no subcontractors" line is not available to us regardless.

Every winner's proof is its own work; the moment we show someone else's, we are Nu Look. This is recorded here so it is not relitigated under competitive pressure.

## Recommended order of work

1. **Get the credential set from Jarrod** — ABN, contractor licence number, public liability sum and insurer, and whether any written warranty exists. Four answers unlock positions 02 and 13, the only beats where every strong competitor beats us outright. Blocked on a person, not on work.
2. **Settle a per-m² range** that survives scrutiny, and put it in the FAQ beside the fixed-written-price promise, the way Dynamic does.
3. **Add one honest "we won't do that."** Epoxy over tiles is the obvious candidate — a competitor sells it as a headline service; Dynamic explains why it delaminates. The most credibility-dense sentence in the whole scrape set.
4. **Caption the before/afters with specs** — system · m² · suburb — and repeat the spec in the adjacent testimonial.
5. **Publish the location pages** once suburb tagging is done. Same conversation as item 1.
6. **Decide what to do with the speed lead** — surface it or bank it, but stop paying for it invisibly.
