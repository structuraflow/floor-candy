# Guides — section plan and content briefs

**Status: not published.** The Guides section exists as a plan, not as pages.
Nothing here can be written honestly without the answers in
`QUESTIONS-FOR-JARROD.md` §3, and publishing ten AI-written articles against a
three-review business would do more harm than good. This file is the brief; the
pages get built after Jarrod answers.

---

## Section decisions

- **Name it "Guides", not "Blog."** "Blog" signals a content mill; "Guides"
  signals reference material, which is what a homeowner researching a $X,000
  floor is actually looking for.
- **Route:** `/guides` for the index, `/guides/<slug>` for each article.
- **Ship order:** publish one guide at a time, each fully answered by Jarrod.
  The index page goes live with the **third** guide — two entries is a thin
  index and looks abandoned.
- **Template:** reuse the existing page chrome (breadcrumbs already exist).
  Each guide gets `Article` structured data **only** if it has a truthful author
  and a real publication date. No fabricated bylines, no fabricated dates, no
  fabricated "reviewed by".
- **Every guide must:** answer the headline question in its first two paragraphs;
  carry at least one genuine Floor Candy photograph; link to the relevant
  service page, a relevant project, and `/floor-assessment`; and end with the
  standard assessment CTA.
- **No guide may:** quote a price, name a product, state a cure time, or claim a
  warranty until §3 is answered.

## The ten briefs

### 1. How Much Does an Epoxy Garage Floor Cost in Forster and Taree?
**Intent:** commercial-investigation, highest volume, highest anxiety.
**Answer up front:** why a single number is not honest, then the variables.
**Needs from Jarrod:** 3.5, 3.9, and a decision on whether to publish a range at all.
**Do not publish** with an invented range. If Jarrod will not publish figures,
this becomes "What Changes the Price of an Epoxy Garage Floor" — same value, no
fabrication.
**Links:** `/garage-floor-coating`, `/floor-assessment`, `/process`.

### 2. How Long Does an Epoxy Garage Floor Last?
**Intent:** durability / risk reduction.
**Answer up front:** lifespan is decided by preparation and by use, not by the
product's label.
**Needs:** 3.2, 3.4, 3.8. Without a warranty position this stays qualitative.
**Angle only Floor Candy can write:** what Jarrod sees when he grinds off a
floor that failed early.

### 3. Why Epoxy Floors Peel: The Importance of Concrete Preparation
**Intent:** problem-aware. **This is the flagship guide** — it is the site's
entire positioning in long form.
**Answer up front:** peeling is a bond failure, and bond is decided before the
coating goes on.
**Needs:** 3.1, 3.3, 3.13. Should carry the grinding photograph and the garage
before/after.
**Links:** `/process`, `#garage-transformation`.

### 4. Epoxy vs Polyaspartic Flooring: What Is the Difference?
**Intent:** comparison, high commercial value.
**Needs:** 3.12 — **blocked** until Jarrod says what he actually uses and
believes. A comparison written without his position is competitor paraphrase.

### 5. Can Epoxy Be Applied Over Old or Damaged Concrete?
**Intent:** qualification — readers with a slab they think is beyond help.
**Answer up front:** usually yes, and the condition changes the preparation, not
whether it can be done.
**Needs:** 3.6, 3.7. Strong internal link to the assessment.

### 6. Best Garage Flooring Options for Coastal Mid North Coast Homes
**Intent:** local + informational. **The most locally defensible guide** and the
one competitors in Newcastle cannot write.
**Covers:** salt air, humidity, slabs that stay damp, holiday homes that sit
closed up, UV on anything outdoors.
**Needs:** 3.3, and one or two genuine coastal job examples.
**Links:** `/service-areas`.

### 7. How to Maintain a Flake Epoxy Garage Floor
**Intent:** post-purchase, but ranks well and builds trust pre-purchase.
**Needs:** Jarrod's actual handover advice — what he tells customers to use and
avoid. Should mirror the handover sheet if one exists.

### 8. Is Epoxy Flooring Suitable for Driveways and Outdoor Areas?
**Intent:** service qualification. Answers a question the site currently only
half-answers.
**Answer up front:** yes, with a system specified for UV and weather — which is
a different specification to an enclosed garage.
**Needs:** 2.4, 3.2. Carries the driveway before/after.

### 9. Metallic Epoxy Flooring: Appearance, Uses and Maintenance
**Status: blocked.** Needs 2.1 **and** genuine metallic photographs. Will not be
written illustrated with the grey flake garage.

### 10. What Happens During a Professional Floor Assessment?
**Intent:** removes friction from the primary conversion action. Short guide,
high conversion value.
**Needs:** 3.11.
**Links:** straight into `/floor-assessment`.
