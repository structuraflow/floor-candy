Why floors fail

Epoxy floors don’t fail. Poor preparation does.

When a coated floor lifts, peels or picks up under hot tyres, the coating is almost never the problem. The problem is what it was asked to stick to.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Still unsure? Call Jarrod on 0435 193 845, Mon-Fri, 7am-7pm.

How much does it cost?

Double garages start from approximately $2,450, and new customers save $250 on their first floor. Your exact price depends on size, the condition of the slab, how much preparation it needs, the finish and where you are. You get it as a fixed written price after a free on-site look.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the floor, its condition and the weather. Preparing a difficult slab can take longer than coating it. You get the timeline for your floor in writing with your quote.

When can I walk and drive on it?

Cure times depend on the system and the temperature on the day. Jarrod tells you exactly when it is ready for feet and when it is ready for the car before he leaves.

Will it peel or lift under hot tyres?

Hot tyre lifting is almost always a preparation failure: a coating rolled onto dusty or sealed concrete never properly bonds. That is why every slab is mechanically ground before anything goes down.

What about oil stains and cracks?

Both get looked at during the free assessment. Cracks are repaired where appropriate before coating. A repair stops a crack being an open path through the floor, but it does not change what the slab is doing structurally, and we tell you which is which before you commit.

Is it slippery when wet?

A smooth gloss finish is more slippery wet than dry. A flake floor has texture through it, which helps. If the floor gets wet often or sits on a slope, tell us and we will talk through the right finish.

Do I need to empty the garage?

The floor has to be clear to be ground and coated. If that is a problem, mention it when you book and we will work it out with you.

Which areas do you cover?

Taree, Forster, Tuncurry, Harrington, Blueys Beach, Pacific Palms and the Mid North Coast are our core areas. We take selected projects in Newcastle and the Hunter, depending on job size and timing. Not sure? Send your postcode.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes. Flake comes in blends of greys, creams, tans and mixes, and solid colours are available too. We bring real samples to the on-site quote so you pick in your own light, not off a screen.

Is there a warranty?

We are not publishing warranty terms until they are written down properly. Ask Jarrod at the quote and he will tell you exactly what he stands behind.
