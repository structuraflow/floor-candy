Why there are no suburbs on these

Most flooring sites caption their gallery with a town. We only publish a detail we can stand behind, and the job records behind these photographs don’t all carry a confirmed suburb yet.

Rather than guess, or quietly attribute a floor to whichever town we’d like to rank for, we leave it off — the same reason we publish what we will and won’t claim. As job records are confirmed, individual project pages will be added here with the service, suburb, floor size, existing condition, preparation performed and finished system — and not before.
