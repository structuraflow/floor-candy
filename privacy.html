Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and what we do not do with it.

What we collect

When you submit a floor assessment request we collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb or postcode, and the details you give us about your floor. If you arrive from an advertisement or a search, we also record basic campaign information so we know which channels are working.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your enquiry, arrange an assessment, and prepare a quote. That is the whole purpose.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, add you to marketing lists you did not ask for, or share it with third parties for their own use.

How it reaches us

Form submissions are delivered by a third-party form service and by email. Those providers process the data on our behalf in order to deliver it.

Access and removal

Email admin@floorcandyepoxy.com and we will tell you what we hold or delete it.
