Overview

Floor Candy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or request a quote, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact / quote request form, we collect the details you provide — name, phone number, email address, project location, and any project information you choose to share. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your quote request is submitted via a third-party form processor (Formspree) solely to deliver your enquiry to our team by email.

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use our site. No personally identifying information is sold or shared as a result.
