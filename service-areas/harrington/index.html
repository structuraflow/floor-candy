Epoxy flooring Harrington

Premium flake and epoxy flooring for garages, homes and suitable commercial spaces in Harrington and the lower Manning.

Get a free quote Call 0435 193 845

Harrington is a small town, and small towns are often badly served for trades like this, the work is real but there is not enough of it in one spot to interest an operator based two hours away. We are close enough that a Harrington job is a normal job.

Being at the river mouth, a lot of the concrete here is exposed and weathered, and driveways and paths come up as often as garages do.

It is also a town where word travels. If a neighbour has had a driveway done and you are considering the same, mention it, doing two nearby properties in one trip is genuinely easier for us and we would rather pass that on.

Local detail

River-mouth exposure

Concrete at Harrington gets sun, salt and wind in combination, and outdoor slabs show it faster than they do further inland. Sealing will not reverse weathering that has already happened, but on a slab that is still sound it closes the surface and makes it far easier to keep clean.

Locally

Who calls us in Harrington

Mostly long-term residents and retirees, and the work skews outdoors: driveways, paths and the concrete around the house rather than garage interiors. A lot of it is concrete that was poured decades ago, never sealed, and has spent every year since in sun and salt wind.

The question we are usually asked here is whether a slab is worth treating at all or whether it is past it. That is a fair question and it deserves a straight answer, which is what the free on-site look is for.

FAQ

Questions about Harrington

Is Harrington too small a job to bother with?

No. Harrington is inside our core service area and is priced as such.

My driveway is weathered rather than cracked, is that fixable?

Often, yes. Surface weathering on a slab that is otherwise sound is exactly what sealing is for. We will look at it and tell you honestly whether it is worth doing.

How do I know if my old driveway is worth sealing?

If the surface is weathered but the slab is structurally sound, sealing is usually worth doing. If the concrete is breaking up or moving, it may not be. We will tell you which one you have, and we will say so if the answer is no.

Can you do two properties in the same street together?

Yes, and it helps. Neighbouring jobs on one trip are more efficient for us, which matters in a town the size of Harrington. Mention it when you call.
