Why floors fail

A cheap floor costs more the second time.

Hot-tyre pickup

Warm tyres lift a weakly bonded coating in patches where you park.

Lifting & peeling

The coating never keyed in — usually acid etching instead of mechanical grinding.

Dusting

A weak surface layer left in place keeps shedding grit.

Straight answers

Before you call.

What does it cost?

It depends on the slab — size, condition, whether there is an existing coating to remove, access and finish. You get a fixed itemised price in writing after the assessment, not a range that moves on the day.

Do you cover my suburb?

Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland and surrounding parts of the Hunter Region. Port Stephens, Cessnock and further out are considered depending on the job.

My DIY kit peeled. Can you fix it?

Yes. The failed coating comes off as part of the preparation rather than being coated over.

When do I pay?

Written scope and quote first. A booking deposit may be required to secure your date and order materials. Every project finishes with a final walkthrough, so you can inspect the completed surface before paying the remaining balance.
